Реч по реч / Reč po reč — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Developer: nikvladimirovic · Contact: nikvladimirovich2@gmail.com

Summary

  • Реч по реч is a word-guessing game for players aged 13 and over, in Serbian (Cyrillic and Latin) and Russian.
  • Your games, statistics and chosen alphabet are saved only on your device. The developer runs no server and receives none of this data.
  • The app has no account, no login, no analytics and no crash-reporting service.
  • The app shows ads served by Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, occasionally a full-screen ad between finished games (never during a game), and an optional video ad you can choose to watch to reveal a letter. The ad SDK is the only part of the app that uses the internet.

Data stored on your device

DataPurpose
Your guesses in the current games, hints used, statistics (games played, wins, streaks)So you can continue a game and see your progress
The alphabet you chose and how many games have passed since the last full-screen adSo the app remembers your choice and limits how often ads appear

This data stays in the app's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app. It may be included in your phone's own Android backup if you have that turned on.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google: your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interactions. Ads are limited to content rated for teens (T) or lower.

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings on the home screen.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

The game itself does not need the internet: without a connection it works fully, just without ads.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.
