One More Beer — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Developer: nikvladimirovic · Contact: nikvladimirovich2@gmail.com

Summary

One More Beer shows one built-in phrase (“One more beer, please”) in several languages, with a transcription and audio.

Data the app stores on your device

DataPurpose
Chosen app language (optional)Show the app in English, Russian or Serbian; stored by Android

This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone and is never sent anywhere by the app.

Audio (text-to-speech)

When you tap ▶, the fixed phrase text is passed to your phone's text-to-speech engine (usually Google's). If no offline voice is installed for that language, that engine may create the audio online under its own privacy terms. The app itself calls no web service for this and sends nothing about you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Google Play version shows a small banner ad provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it. According to Google's data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device;
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance;
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. The app does not pass any of its own content (counts, settings, choices) to the ad SDK.

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where required, Google's consent form is shown before personalised ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google Privacy Policy.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a technical report (app version, Android version, error details) is saved on your device only. On the next launch the app offers to share it with the developer; it is sent only if you choose to, using an app you pick (for example e-mail). You can also delete it. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

What we do not do

  • No account, login or registration.
  • No server of our own; we (the developer) cannot see any of your data.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of our own, no sale of data.
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • App data is excluded from Android cloud backup.

Children

The app is intended only for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed to children.

Data deletion and your rights

All app data stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Because we hold no personal data on any server, there is no account to delete. Data collected by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID and manage ad settings in Android and at myadcenter.google.com. Depending on where you live (for example GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, delete or object; contact us at nikvladimirovich2@gmail.com.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised.

One More Beer is for entertainment only. Please drink responsibly, and never drive after drinking.
