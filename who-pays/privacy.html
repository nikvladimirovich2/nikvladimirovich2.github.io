Who Pays — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026 · Developer: nikvladimirovic · Contact: nikvladimirovich2@gmail.com

Summary. Who Pays is a set of party games for a company at one table (Guess the number, Spy, Perudo, Lowest unique). The phones of the players connect to the developer's game server for the duration of a game. There are no accounts. The server keeps a game only in memory while it is played and deletes it right after.

Data the app stores on your device

DataPurpose
Nickname (optional)Shown to the other players of a game; you can change or clear it in Settings
Chosen app language (optional)Show the app in English, Russian or Serbian
Current game session (room code and a random session key)Return to a running game after a short connection loss; deleted when the game ends

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is excluded from Android cloud backup.

Data sent to the game server

To play together, the app sends to the developer's game server (hosted on Google Cloud in the USA):

DataWhen and why
Room code and a random session keyTo create, join or rejoin a game
NicknameOnly after every player has joined, so the others see who is who. If you leave it empty, "Player N" is used
Your moves (numbers, bids, votes and similar)To play the game; secret moves are never shown to the other players before the game reveals them
IP addressReceived with every internet connection; used only in memory to limit the number of connections and wrong room codes per address (protection against abuse)

The connection is encrypted (TLS). The server keeps all of this only in memory and deletes a game as soon as it is over (at the latest after 30 minutes without activity). It does not write nicknames, moves or IP addresses to logs or to any database; its technical logs contain only room codes, game phases, counts and error codes. This data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Google Play version shows a small banner ad provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it. According to Google's data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK automatically collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of the device;
  • Product interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance;
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. The app does not pass any game content (nicknames, moves) to the ad SDK.

Consent: in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where required, Google's consent form is shown before personalised ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options.
Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google Privacy Policy.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a technical report (app version, Android version, error details) is saved on your device only. On the next launch the app offers to share it with the developer; it is sent only if you choose to, using an app you pick (for example e-mail). You can also delete it. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

What we do not do

  • No account, login or registration; no chat.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs of our own, no sale of data.
  • No access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Data deletion and your rights

App data on your device is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Game data on the server is deleted automatically when the game ends; there is no account to delete. Data collected by Google for advertising is governed by Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID and manage ad settings in Android and at myadcenter.google.com. Depending on where you live (for example GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, delete or object; contact us at nikvladimirovich2@gmail.com.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised.
