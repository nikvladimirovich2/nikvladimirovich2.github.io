Blocks & Critters — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Developer: nikvladimirovic · Contact: nikvladimirovich2@gmail.com

Summary

Blocks & Critters is a block puzzle game for the whole family (6 and up, and adults). As of this version, it does the following and nothing else:

  • It saves your progress — scores, eggs, the critters you have found, your settings and your chosen language — only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • It does not use the internet. It has no ads, no account, no login, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no chat, and no way to contact other people through the app.
  • The only way out of the app is a link to the Google Play Store page for the app itself (for example to leave a rating); there is no other external link.

In short: the app collects no data, transmits no data, and works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

DataPurpose
Your scores, eggs, and the critters you have collectedSo progress is kept between sessions
Your chosen settings (sound, vibration, less motion, high contrast, theme, language)So the app remembers your preferences

All of this is stored only in the app's own private storage on your phone, in a format only the app itself can read, and is deleted automatically when you uninstall the app, or at once if you use the in-app "Reset progress" button. Nobody — including the developer — can see this data; we do not operate a server and the app has no way to send it to one.

Children

The app is designed to be enjoyable for a 6-year-old playing alone and for adults alike. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, and this will not change without this page being updated first. Any part of the app meant only for a parent (for example "Reset progress") is behind a simple arithmetic question that a young child cannot answer by guessing or tapping randomly.

No ads right now

Earlier test builds of this app experimented with advertising (Google AdMob). That code has been removed after it caused the app to crash on launch, and there is no ad SDK, no advertising-ID permission, and no ad-related network call in this version. If ads are reintroduced in a future version, this page will be updated first, together with the required Google Play Data safety form, before that version is published.

What we do not do

  • No account, login or registration, of any kind.
  • No server of our own, and no third-party server either (no ads, no analytics, no cloud saves).
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or any other permission beyond writing to the app's own private storage.
  • No data is sold, shared, or used for anything beyond showing you your own saved game.
  • App data is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.

Data deletion

All data stays on your device. Using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes your scores, eggs, critters and designs at once (your language and settings choices are kept, as described in the app). Uninstalling the app deletes everything. There is no account to delete, because there is no account.

Changes

If what the app collects or does ever changes — most likely if advertising is reintroduced — this page will be updated first, with a new effective date, before that version reaches the Play Store.
