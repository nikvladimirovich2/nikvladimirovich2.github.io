Blocks & Critters — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Developer: nikvladimirovic · Contact: nikvladimirovich2@gmail.com

Summary

Blocks & Critters is a block puzzle game for the whole family (6 and up, and adults).

  • It saves your progress — scores, eggs, the critters you have found, your settings and your chosen language — only on your device. Nothing about your play is ever sent anywhere.
  • It shows one small banner ad, on the home screen only (never during play), served by Google AdMob. That is the app's only use of the internet.
  • Every ad request is marked as directed at a mixed audience that includes children, so Google serves only non-personalised, contextual ads, rated suitable for general audiences (G), and never collects or uses an advertising ID — the app does not even request that permission.
  • It has no account, no login, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, no chat, and no way to contact other people through the app.
  • The only way out of the app is a link to the Google Play Store page for the app itself (for example to leave a rating); there is no other external link.

In short: the app itself collects and transmits no personal data, and is fully playable offline; the only network traffic is the one home-screen ad banner.

Data stored on your device

DataPurpose
Your scores, eggs, and the critters you have collectedSo progress is kept between sessions
Your chosen settings (sound, vibration, less motion, high contrast, theme, language)So the app remembers your preferences

All of this is stored only in the app's own private storage on your phone, in a format only the app itself can read, and is deleted automatically when you uninstall the app, or at once if you use the in-app "Reset progress" button. Nobody — including the developer — can see this data; we do not operate a server and the app has no way to send it to one.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The home screen shows one small banner ad, provided by Google AdMob. This is the only part of the app that uses the internet.

  • Every ad request this app makes is tagged as child-directed. Google's AdMob SDK responds to that tag by serving only non-personalised, contextually-chosen ads rated for general audiences, and by not using the advertising ID or any other identifier to build a profile of the viewer.
  • The app does not request the Android advertising-ID permission at all, so it could not share that identifier even if it tried to.
  • Google, as the ad provider, may still process basic, non-personal technical information needed to serve and measure any ad (such as a general, coarse region derived from IP address, device type, and ad performance counts) under its own role as an ad-serving provider. See Google's own disclosures: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431 (child-directed treatment).
  • No ad is shown anywhere except the home screen; the puzzle itself, Zen mode, and Daily Puzzle are always ad-free.
  • If the device is offline, or no ad is available, the app simply shows no ad in that spot — the game is always fully playable without one.

Children

The app is designed to be enjoyable for a 6-year-old playing alone and for adults alike. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. Every ad shown is treated as shown to a child, regardless of who is actually playing, which is a deliberately more restrictive setting than the law requires for any adult player. Any part of the app meant only for a parent (for example "Reset progress") is behind a simple arithmetic question that a young child cannot answer by guessing or tapping randomly.

What we do not do

  • No account, login or registration, of any kind.
  • No server of our own, and no third-party server beyond the one ad SDK described above (no analytics, no cloud saves, no social features).
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or any other permission beyond writing to the app's own private storage and the internet access the ad SDK needs to fetch an ad.
  • No data about your play, your progress, or anything you do in the game is ever sold, shared, or sent anywhere.
  • App data is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.

Data deletion

All of your game data stays on your device. Using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes your scores, eggs, critters and designs at once (your language and settings choices are kept, as described in the app). Uninstalling the app deletes everything. There is no account to delete, because there is no account.

Changes

If what the app collects or does ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date, before that version reaches the Play Store.
